## 👋 About me

I'm **Felix Odhiambo** (people call me *Felloh*), a software engineer in Nairobi, Kenya.

In plain English: **I build websites, mobile apps, and the invisible plumbing that keeps them running.** I've been doing it for over twelve years — starting by taking Android phones apart, and ending up building payment systems, everyday apps, and products with a bit of AI inside.

**What I can help with**

| | |
|---|---|
| 🌍 **Websites & web apps** | From a simple landing page to a full customer portal — fast on any connection. |
| 📱 **Mobile apps** | Android and cross-platform apps, from idea to the app store. |
| 💳 **Payments** | I wrote a free M-PESA tool that other teams reuse to take mobile money. |
| 🤖 **AI features** | Chat assistants and smart search added to real products. |
| ☁️ **Hosting & upkeep** | Servers and automatic updates, so your product keeps running while you sleep. |

👉 **[Have a look at my website](https://fellow3-1.github.io/Fellow3-1/)** — it has a light and a dark mode, and the dark one is proper AMOLED black.
